What we do

Support for everyday life at home

Services are shaped around each person's needs, preferences and service plan.

01 / CARE

Personal care & daily living

  • Bathing, hygiene, grooming and dressing
  • Toileting and continence assistance
  • Mobility and transfer support within the service plan
  • Meal setup and eating assistance
  • Medication reminders within our non-medical scope
02 / HOME

Home & homemaking support

  • Routine housekeeping and cleaning
  • Laundry and linen support
  • Kitchen and bathroom cleaning
  • Organization and light decluttering
  • Meal preparation and household assistance
03 / CONNECTION

Companionship & practical support

  • Companionship and meaningful social support
  • Shopping and errands
  • Transportation support when arranged
  • Community participation and everyday activities
  • Support that respects routines, culture and personal choices
